# Asset sources

Technology brand icons are from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/c37b20d397a710fdd402aae3cb643b820b142074/icons), distributed under CC0-1.0. Their colours are adapted for contrast on the portfolio’s pastel backgrounds. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners.

SQL, IndexedDB, and REST API use locally drawn generic symbols.

The About portrait is Vernit Gupta's [public GitHub profile picture](https://avatars.githubusercontent.com/u/225435077?v=4), added at his request on 15 November 2026. The original is stored as `dist/assets/vernit-gupta.jpg`; circular framing is applied in CSS.

The retained DescentLab reference screenshot and brand mark are from [Vernit Gupta's DescentLab repository](https://github.com/guptavernit2-afk/descent-lab/tree/5dca1c20c6ff1b4411d3ac9227f4c0d8dbbe18fc). The retained MediWatch reference screenshot is from its [repository](https://github.com/guptavernit2-afk/MediWatch).

Current project imagery consists of actual application and simulator captures. CatchUp was captured from its live workspace using the built-in sample conversation in Instant Rules mode. DescentLab and MediWatch use focused crops of the repository screenshots described above. Rover Fetch uses the team repository's [simulator arena image](https://github.com/parasmani-dev/hackoween/blob/main/rover_lab/site/arena.jpg). Avertiq uses the existing screenshot of its published training dashboard, retrieved through Sites. See [capture sources and crop records](docs/project-screenshots.md).

Avertiq's map contains © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright) geography and [Mapterhorn terrain](https://mapterhorn.com/attribution). Credits are displayed beside the preview and in its detail dialog. The displayed incident inputs and assessments are illustrative training data.

The earlier generated concept illustrations are no longer published. Their prompts remain as historical documentation in [project preview history](docs/project-3d-previews.md) and [Avertiq illustration history](docs/avertiq-illustration.md).
